# Formats

## Post (feed)

- 1,300–2,000 characters; the first ~210 show before "…see more", so the hook and its reason both land there.
- Shape:
  1. Hook — one line naming the reader's problem.
  2. Two to three lines of mechanism: what happens and why it costs.
  3. The numbers, as emoji or arrow bullets, with one worked example.
  4. What the thing does — three to five bullets, each `emoji Label: one clause`.
  5. "Why it matters" — three `→` lines.
  6. One closing line that generalises ("You can't manage what you can't see.").
  7. "Link in comments." when a link is allowed at all — links in the body cut reach.
  8. A question with 👇, then the hashtags.
- One image: a screenshot of the real thing beats a generated picture.

## Article

- Title: a claim, not a topic ("Ask Once, Then Get Out of the Way"), under 100 characters.
- 1,000–1,800 words, six to eight `##` sections, each one idea.
- Open with the two failure modes the reader has seen; close with "Why this matters" as four short lines and the question.
- Lists for anything with three or more parallel items; bold for the one sentence a skimmer must not miss.
- Cover 1920×1080; the centre must survive a crop to 1200×630.

## Cover-image prompt

Output one prompt and nothing else — no variants, no per-tool settings table, no tips. The prompt carries its own technical description, in this order:

1. **Brief** — one sentence: what the image is for and the idea it shows.
2. **Scene** — the setting, camera angle (e.g. 30° isometric), and the left-to-right flow the eye follows.
3. **Subjects** — each element, its shape, material and what it is doing.
4. **Style** — rendering technique (flat-isometric 3D, vector-clean, soft volumetric light), reference aesthetic.
5. **Palette** — background and accent colours as hex.
6. **Composition** — aspect ratio 16:9, 1920×1080, subject per third, crop-safe centre 1200×630, negative space for an optional title.
7. **Lighting** — key, rim and fill, with their colours.
8. **Quality** — resolution, focus, detail level.
9. **Exclusions** — a closing "No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks or brand names", since generators garble text.

A title belongs in Figma or Canva afterwards, never in the prompt.
